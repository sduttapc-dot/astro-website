Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide personal readings and consultations, we collect:

  • Contact details: Name, phone/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Birth details: Date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth.
  • Transaction identifiers generated during payment via Razorpay (we do not store credit/debit card credentials directly on our servers).

2. Use of Information

Your personal data is used solely to generate your Vedic birth charts, coordinate consultations, provide remedial advice, and share invoices/receipts.

3. Confidentiality Guarantee

We adhere to strict 100% privacy principles. Your birth data, questions, personal discussions, and consultation audio/video recordings will never be traded, sold, or shared with third-party advertising brokers.

4. Secure Payments

Payment processing is routed through PCI-DSS certified gateway providers (Razorpay). Sensitive card credentials or UPI passwords are never handled or stored on AstroVedic servers.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions regarding your personal information, email us at contact@astrovedic.com.
