Today's Nakshatra & Vedic Cosmic Energy

Lunar mansion coordinates, ruling planet, planetary deity, and recommended karmic actions

Rohini Nakshatra

Active Window: Throughout the Day & Night (4th Lunar Mansion)

Deity: Lord Brahma (Prajapati)

Planetary Ruler & Sign

Ruling Planet: Chandra (Moon)

Associated Zodiac: Vrishabha (Taurus)

Gana: Manushya (Human)

Energy & Symbol

Symbol: Ox Cart / Chariot

Nature: Dhruva (Fixed, Stable & Permanent)

Significance: Fosters beauty, creativity, agricultural wealth, and material fertility.

Auspicious Remedies

Beneficial Color: White / Off-White

Mantra: Om Rohinye Namah

Remedy: Offer fresh water or milk to a Shiva Lingam for mental clarity.

Activities Favorable & Unfavorable under Rohini

  • Favorable: Foundation laying for buildings, purchasing vehicle or luxury goods, starting businesses, and wedding ceremonies.
  • Favorable: Wearing new gems, agricultural plantation, artistic creations, and long-term financial investments.
  • Avoid: Destruction, demolition of structures, or entering harsh confrontational arguments.
  • Avoid: Taking high-risk short-term speculative decisions without proper financial calculation.
